#### The nine steps

##### 1. Reconcile the brief and define design success

Read the current step from the brief. Freeze a source snapshot with stable brief line IDs, page/template inventory, audience, principal job, conversion or task goal, known constraints and decision status. Resolve repository, stack, design tool, roles and languages from settings. Write observable success criteria for each visitor task. Write one proposed visual thesis: “For [audience] doing [job], the design makes [brief-backed distinction] visible through [hierarchy/content behavior].” This is internal rationale, not website copy. Cite the exact brief lines for each clause. Specify two or three operational consequences, such as making the next action prominent or putting evidence beside a decision. A decorative adjective alone is not a point of view. Exit when the source hierarchy, scope and decision map are unambiguous enough for content work.

##### 2. Visitor needs, content and evidence

Copy comes before layout. Take approved copy from the brief or the project's writer; preserve exact approved wording. Build the copy deck with a stable slot ID, screen/state, purpose, text, language/direction, character range, tone note, source lines and all fact references. Every claim, statistic, named customer, logo and quotation needs a confirmed project fact and any required consent. Source presence alone is not confirmation. Record who checked it, when and against which source revision. A testimonial also needs its exact wording, speaker and permission.

Use visible `[placeholder]` for a missing line, listing its slot, owner and required source. Never create a plausible headline, number or customer as temporary content. Placeholders may support an explicitly incomplete wireframe; they fail Step 9. Give the writer a text-fit request when a slot is too long; widen or reflow the layout first. Do not cut copy, ellipsize content or invent a shorter claim to fit a box.

Collect image briefs describing the communication job, required content, rights and alt-text intent. Identify what not to show. Exit with an inventory that accounts for every slot, source, missing item and consent requirement. Known blocking content gaps remain visible.

##### 3. Information architecture

Map each page and section to a visitor task and an exact brief line. Each section brief names its purpose, content slots, evidence, CTA and destination, expected state changes and success check. Remove sections with no job. A structural decision, grid break, illustration or motion proposal gets the same trace, so the critic can reject anything without one.

Build a focused reference board of three real, inspected references: one useful for hierarchy, one for content density/type and one for the relevant interaction. These are comparison roles, not required vendors or aesthetic templates. Record URL, inspection date, local evidence where rights permit, what works, what would be wrong for this project and the relevant brief lines. Do not copy a reference's text, assets or trademark presentation. If a reference was inaccessible, mark it untested and replace it before the critic relies on it.

Exit with the complete sitemap, task flows, section briefs and a single recommended direction. Reference selection must support the project's point of view, not impose a fashionable palette.

##### 4. Grayscale desktop wireframes

Annotate column spans, reading order, grouping, content-driven height, intended media job, interaction/state behavior and brief references. Check the project's other permitted desktop widths. A centered section means its principal heading, copy and CTA are compositionally centered as a unit; centered images or cells alone do not count. Permit at most one such section per page. Do not evade the count by replacing semantic sections with anonymous containers.

##### 6. Desktop visual design

Only after Step 5 approval, develop the chosen point of view with type, spacing, color, assets and motion. Start with the project's approved tokens and components. A new brand token still requires the existing approval. Pair display and reading roles deliberately; a single family with a justified role contrast is valid. Select fonts only after checking the project's language coverage, weight availability, licence and real text rendering.

Use at most **six distinct rendered type sizes per page**: display, title, section heading, body, secondary text and label. Six supplies a useful hierarchy without arbitrary intermediate steps; fewer is preferable when sufficient. Document font family, fallback, size, line height, weight and tracking for each role. Weight and spacing carry distinctions before another size is added. The six-size ceiling applies within each locale; translations may use a separately approved metric-equivalent scale with the same ceiling. Text must remain readable at user zoom.

Use a named spacing scale and specify the grid, margins, gutters, alignment lines and section rhythm in tokens. Every intentional grid break identifies the brief-backed reason and the reading-order consequence. Do not fabricate irregularity to appear human. Use neutrals and one strong accent; accent-bearing area must be at most ten percent in both the full-page composition and the reviewed desktop viewport. Semantic colors may communicate states but require text or shape as well; they are not extra decorative accents. The screenshot review catches color inside media and effects that DOM measurements miss.

Avoid a default purple-blue combination, generic gradient hero, stock glass effects or repeated rounded-card grid. An existing brand conflict is reported for a decision, not silently rebranded. Each visual and icon must help someone understand, navigate, decide or recognize something. Remove it when that job cannot be named.

For each motion, record the job, trigger, duration in milliseconds, easing, affected elements, interruption/cleanup behavior and static fallback. Under reduced motion there is no movement. Essential content remains visible with JavaScript disabled; the project's specified script-failure deadline is an acceptance check. Motion has no value merely because a page feels quiet without it.

This synthetic text is test input, never publishable copy. Character ranges are planning guidance: if an approved translation exceeds one, revise the range and layout rather than truncate. Use logical start/end layout rules; check mixed numbers, punctuation, links and directional icons in RTL. Never mirror logos or text blindly. ## 3. Integrated anti-AI design and copy checklist

### Integrated design and copy acceptance

Load at section planning, the Step 4/5 grayscale review and the Step 8 pre-handoff check. This is the rulebook for `tools/pre-handoff-check.mjs`, not an optional separate review. The machine thresholds are in `runtime/quality-checks.json`; semantic verdicts and evidence live in the same `handoff.json` used by the checker. A missing required manual check fails acceptance. Automated detectors identify measurable patterns; a human/independent critic checks meaning and visual exceptions. Neither is an authorship detector. Impeccable findings are advisory: its `pass` means the pinned detector ran and produced parseable output, not that it found no design problems. The gate separately requires the scoped critic/manual PASS records for generic heroes, rounded-card grids, dashboard tropes, decoration and other semantic criteria; absent or failed records block handoff.

#### Measurement contract

The reference viewport is 1440 × 900 CSS pixels at device scale one, with a full-page screenshot as well. Visit every declared screen and applicable state. Wait for local fonts and assets; log failed or blocked loads. Keep external requests, service workers and WebSockets blocked during the local check. Capture file hashes and the version under test. A reference PNG is 1440 pixels wide and named `screen--state--locale--vNN.png`; its hash identifies the version. A screenshot from another revision cannot support a PASS.

Mark visible text wrappers with `data-copy-slot="ID"` and match every displayed string to the approved locale variant in the copy deck, including interface and error labels. Unlisted or mismatched text blocks final handoff. Declare semantic section, component, copy-slot and media IDs in the reference DOM and match them to the handoff inventory. The critic inspects the full screenshot and semantic outline so unmarked containers cannot evade a count. CSS/computed-style counts are recorded as measurements. Layout intent, claims, tasteful restraint and the purpose of an image remain reviewed judgments with an artifact, reviewer, explanation and brief line references. A guessed PASS flag is not evidence.

Where the browser test uses a proxy, label it as a proxy in `evidence.json`. In particular, DOM element rectangles do not measure the actual colored pixels of text, images, gradients or overlapping regions. For accent coverage, the documented acceptance measure is visible accent area divided by total rendered area, separately for the full page and each reviewed viewport. The reviewer supplies an annotated color mask or measured screenshot regions for image/canvas/SVG content and any disputed DOM estimate. Use union area, not summed overlapping boxes. Borderline or unmeasurable coverage remains FAIL pending evidence; do not round 10.1% down to 10%.

#### Pass/fail checklist

Every row is BLOCK unless explicitly marked WARN. `wireframe` evaluates the structure/copy column; visual-only criteria are recorded as deferred, never called PASS. `handoff` requires both columns and all fourteen manual IDs listed below.

#### Manual evidence that the checker requires

`manualChecks` includes `point-of-view`, `brief-trace`, `generic-hero`, `decoration-job`, `card-grid`, `dashboard-tropes`, `rule-of-three`, `reference-work`, `craft`, `wcag-review`, `accent-area`, `palette`, `rules-copy` and `language-overflow`. Every record identifies its screen, language and state, PASS/FAIL, reviewer, artifact path, brief line references and a concrete note. An artifact may cover several states, but each record identifies the part supporting its exact scope. The artifact contains the actual rubric/check result, screenshot references and measured values where relevant. Missing coverage for a screen is a failure even if another screen passed. A grayscale artifact records visual tests as deferred while judging structural craft; it does not sign off final color or typography.

The critic asks: with the company name masked, does the headline still express a precise offering, audience task or independently sourced distinction from this brief? Record the compared alternative categories and the reason. If it could be pasted unchanged onto arbitrary company sites, fail and return the slot to the writer. Do not replace an approved headline without the existing approval process.

For functional dashboards, derive content from the user's decisions and workflows. Use a ranked queue, trend or table when that is what the task requires; do not add a summary-card row merely because dashboards often have one. Every number requires a real source, scope and update meaning. Static sample data must be explicitly labelled as a fixture and cannot enter a real handoff as production data. “Live” requires a real documented data path; animation is not evidence.

#### Accessibility evidence, accurately scoped

Record each text foreground/background pair and state. Normal text needs 4.5:1 contrast; large text needs 3:1 using WCAG's definition of large text. Contrast uses specified/computed colors, not antialiased screenshot edges. Do not round a failing ratio upward. [W3C contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html).

Applicable control boundaries, meaningful icons and graphical objects need 3:1 against adjacent colors. Record their actual role and applicable exceptions rather than claiming every decorative border must pass. Status also has a readable label or shape. [W3C non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html).

Record sequential keyboard focus, visible focus before/after, modal entry/escape/return, traps and sticky overlays. AA focus-not-obscured requires that the focused control is not entirely hidden by author content; prefer full visibility as this project's stricter usability target. Focus Appearance 2.4.13 is AAA, so do not mislabel it as the AA criterion. [W3C focus-not-obscured](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum).

Measure actual pointer targets. A 24 × 24 CSS-pixel target is the baseline; any smaller target needs a documented qualifying spacing, equivalent-control, inline, user-agent or essential exception. Bounding boxes and an “inline” tag are only screening heuristics. [W3C target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum).

Test non-dragging alternatives for dragging operations, and accessible authentication without an unsupported cognitive test where authentication exists. If absent, record N/A with the screen inventory that proves absence. [W3C WCAG 2.2 additions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/).

Check 200% text resizing and 400% desktop zoom/reflow (equivalent 320 CSS-pixel width), including applicable two-dimensional content exceptions. This is accessibility validation, not authorization to design phone layouts. Document browser zoom and viewport used. [W3C reflow guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html).

Set the page language, mark language changes and specify base direction separately. Test real RTL content, mixed-direction identifiers, punctuation and numbers; use appropriate directional markup rather than reversing strings. [W3C internationalization guidance](https://www.w3.org/International/questions/qa-html-dir).

### `tools/local-file-boundary.mjs`

````javascript
import { readFileSync, realpathSync, statSync } from 'node:fs';
import { extname, sep } from 'node:path';
import { fileURLToPath } from 'node:url';

const types = { '.html': 'text/html; charset=utf-8', '.css': 'text/css; charset=utf-8', '.js': 'text/javascript; charset=utf-8', '.json': 'application/json', '.png': 'image/png', '.jpg': 'image/jpeg', '.jpeg': 'image/jpeg', '.svg': 'image/svg+xml', '.woff': 'font/woff', '.woff2': 'font/woff2' };
export function localAsset(url, projectRoot) {
  const parsed = new URL(url);
  if (parsed.protocol !== 'file:' || parsed.search || parsed.hash) throw Error('only local project files are allowed');
  const root = realpathSync(projectRoot), path = realpathSync(fileURLToPath(parsed));
  if (!path.startsWith(root + sep) || !statSync(path).isFile()) throw Error('file request is outside the selected project');
  const contentType = types[extname(path).toLowerCase()];
  if (!contentType) throw Error('file type is not allowed');
  return { body: readFileSync(path), contentType, path };
}
````

### `tools/safe-write.py`

````python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""Linux-only, descriptor-relative output writer. Never removes a pathname."""
import ctypes
import os
import platform
from pathlib import Path
import re
import stat
import sys

ROOT = Path('/workspace/design-output')
SLUG = re.compile(r'[a-z0-9](?:[a-z0-9-]{0,62}[a-z0-9])?\Z')
SYS_OPENAT2 = 437  # Linux x86_64 and aarch64
RESOLVE_NO_SYMLINKS = 0x04
RESOLVE_BENEATH = 0x08


class OpenHow(ctypes.Structure):
    _fields_ = [('flags', ctypes.c_ulonglong), ('mode', ctypes.c_ulonglong), ('resolve', ctypes.c_ulonglong)]


def openat2(dirfd, path, flags, mode=0):
    how = OpenHow(flags, mode, RESOLVE_NO_SYMLINKS | RESOLVE_BENEATH)
    libc = ctypes.CDLL(None, use_errno=True)
    fd = libc.syscall(SYS_OPENAT2, dirfd, os.fsencode(path), ctypes.byref(how), ctypes.sizeof(how))
    if fd < 0:
        code = ctypes.get_errno()
        raise OSError(code, os.strerror(code), path)
    return fd


def validate_target(root, project, target):
    if len(project) > 64 or not SLUG.fullmatch(project):
        raise ValueError('invalid project slug')
    if not target.startswith('/') or any(p in ('.', '..') for p in target.split('/')):
        raise ValueError('absolute target without dot segments required')
    parts = Path(target).parts
    prefix = root.parts + (project,)
    if parts[:len(prefix)] != prefix or len(parts) <= len(prefix):
        raise ValueError('target must be a file beneath the named project')
    return parts[len(prefix):]


def require_host(root):
    if sys.platform != 'linux' or platform.machine() not in ('x86_64', 'aarch64'):
        raise RuntimeError('secure writer requires Linux x86_64/aarch64 with openat2')
    if os.geteuid() == 0:
        raise RuntimeError('run as a dedicated unprivileged user')
    for path in (Path('/workspace'), root):
        s = os.lstat(path)
        if not stat.S_ISDIR(s.st_mode) or s.st_uid != 0 or s.st_mode & 0o022:
            raise RuntimeError(f'{path} must be root-owned without group/other write')
    fd = os.open(root, os.O_RDONLY | os.O_DIRECTORY | os.O_NOFOLLOW | os.O_CLOEXEC)
    try:
        probe = openat2(fd, '.', os.O_RDONLY | os.O_DIRECTORY | os.O_CLOEXEC)
        os.close(probe)
    finally:
        os.close(fd)


def checked_dir(project_fd, relative):
    fd = openat2(project_fd, relative, os.O_RDONLY | os.O_DIRECTORY | os.O_CLOEXEC)
    s = os.fstat(fd)
    if s.st_uid != os.geteuid() or s.st_mode & 0o022:
        os.close(fd)
        raise RuntimeError('project output directory has untrusted owner or permissions')
    for marker in ('.git', 'HEAD'):
        try:
            os.stat(marker, dir_fd=fd, follow_symlinks=False)
        except FileNotFoundError:
            continue
        if marker == '.git' or (marker == 'HEAD' and os.path.isdir(f'/proc/self/fd/{fd}/objects')):
            os.close(fd)
            raise RuntimeError('repository is read-only')
    return fd


def safe_write(root, project, target, content):
    parts = validate_target(root, project, target)
    require_host(root)
    root_fd = os.open(root, os.O_RDONLY | os.O_DIRECTORY | os.O_NOFOLLOW | os.O_CLOEXEC)
    try:
        project_fd = openat2(root_fd, project, os.O_RDONLY | os.O_DIRECTORY | os.O_CLOEXEC)
        try:
            fd = checked_dir(project_fd, '.')
            os.close(fd)
            for i, name in enumerate(parts[:-1]):
                parent = '/'.join(parts[:i]) or '.'
                parent_fd = checked_dir(project_fd, parent)
                try:
                    try:
                        os.mkdir(name, 0o700, dir_fd=parent_fd)
                    except FileExistsError:
                        pass
                finally:
                    os.close(parent_fd)
                child_fd = checked_dir(project_fd, '/'.join(parts[:i + 1]))
                os.close(child_fd)
            parent_fd = checked_dir(project_fd, '/'.join(parts[:-1]) or '.')
            try:
                # One kernel resolution creates the file beneath the pinned project FD.
                # O_EXCL denies overwrites and symlinks. No pathname cleanup follows errors.
                fd = openat2(project_fd, '/'.join(parts), os.O_WRONLY | os.O_CREAT | os.O_EXCL | os.O_CLOEXEC | os.O_NOFOLLOW, 0o600)
                try:
                    view = memoryview(content)
                    while view:
                        written = os.write(fd, view)
                        if written == 0:
                            raise OSError('write made no progress')
                        view = view[written:]
                    os.fsync(fd)
                finally:
                    os.close(fd)
            finally:
                os.close(parent_fd)
        finally:
            os.close(project_fd)
    finally:
        os.close(root_fd)


if __name__ == '__main__':
    if len(sys.argv) != 3:
        print('usage: safe-write.py <project> <absolute-path> < data', file=sys.stderr)
        sys.exit(2)
    try:
        safe_write(ROOT, sys.argv[1], sys.argv[2], sys.stdin.buffer.read())
    except (OSError, ValueError, RuntimeError) as exc:
        print(f'DENY: {exc}', file=sys.stderr)
        sys.exit(15)
    print(f'WROTE: {sys.argv[2]}')
````

### `tools/verify-deps.mjs`

````javascript
// Verify the externally installed npm tree against the reviewed release pin before loading its code.
import { readFileSync, readdirSync, lstatSync, readlinkSync, realpathSync } from 'node:fs';
import { join, relative, sep } from 'node:path';
import { createHash } from 'node:crypto';

const sha = bytes => createHash('sha256').update(bytes).digest('hex');
export function inventoryDigest(root) {
  root = realpathSync(root);
  const entries = [];
  function walk(dir, prefix = '') {
    for (const name of readdirSync(dir).sort()) {
      const path = join(dir, name), rel = prefix ? `${prefix}/${name}` : name, st = lstatSync(path);
      if (st.isDirectory()) { entries.push(`d ${rel}`); walk(path, rel); }
      else if (st.isFile()) entries.push(`f ${rel} ${sha(readFileSync(path))}`);
      else if (st.isSymbolicLink()) {
        const target = readlinkSync(path), resolved = realpathSync(path);
        if (resolved !== root && !resolved.startsWith(root + sep)) throw Error(`dependency symlink escapes tree: ${rel}`);
        entries.push(`l ${rel} ${target}`);
      } else throw Error(`unsupported dependency entry: ${rel}`);
    }
  }
  walk(root);
  return sha(entries.join('\n') + '\n');
}

export function verifyDeps(prefix, pin, releaseRoot) {
  if (!prefix || lstatSync(prefix).isSymbolicLink()) throw Error('dependency prefix missing or symlinked');
  const root = realpathSync(prefix), release = realpathSync(releaseRoot);
  if (root === release || root.startsWith(release + sep)) throw Error('dependency prefix must be outside release');
  const lock = join(root, 'package-lock.json');
  if (sha(readFileSync(lock)) !== pin.lockSha256) throw Error('dependency lock mismatch');
  if (inventoryDigest(join(root, 'node_modules')) !== pin.inventorySha256) throw Error('dependency inventory mismatch');
  return root;
}

export function verifiedDeps(prefix, releaseRoot) {
  const pin = JSON.parse(readFileSync(new URL('../runtime/dependency-trust.json', import.meta.url), 'utf8'));
  const ownLock = sha(readFileSync(new URL('../runtime/package-lock.json', import.meta.url)));
  if (pin.lockSha256 !== ownLock) throw Error('release lock differs from dependency trust pin');
  return verifyDeps(prefix, pin, releaseRoot);
}
````

The critic is independent and read-only. Load the frozen brief, rules, settings, copy/fact ledgers, reference board, scoped screen/state renders and integrated quality evidence. Do not start from the designer's self-assessment. Evaluate each page and all scoped states/locales against their real reference work; check a composition both at full-page scale and at readable 1440-pixel size. Missing evidence is FAIL, not benefit of the doubt.

#### Score anchors and pass rule

Use zero-to-four scores only to make iteration visible: **0** absent or contradicted; **1** recognizable intent with major defects; **2** functional but generic or materially incomplete; **3** clear, specific and supported with all required checks passing; **4** unusually resolved craft, supported by concrete comparisons and detail. A score of four needs an explanation, not enthusiasm.

Every row and every page receives a public **PASS or FAIL**, a score, an artifact reference and a reason. PASS requires at least 3 in every applicable row, all hard checks passing and no open P0/P1. Scores are never averaged to compensate for a failed dimension. A WARN can remain only with a named owner and explicit disposition and no unanswered build question.

| Dimension | PASS at 3 requires | Evidence / automatic failure |
|---|---|---|
| Brief and point of view | One legible idea serving this audience/job, expressed consistently across the page | Brief lines for every section and deliberate decision; any orphan section fails |
| Copy specificity and truth | Accurate approved wording, identifiable offering/task and confirmed proof | Slot/source/claim ledger, project-wording checks; transferable headline, invented proof or filler fails |
| Content hierarchy and task flow | Reading order, emphasis and CTA help the named task; first impression matches actual content | Task walkthrough and annotated frame; vague hero over icon cards or misleading primary action fails |
| Typography and language | Deliberate role pairing, at most six sizes, readable measures, correct scripts/directions and uncut translations | Token-to-DOM mapping, language renders, long-text/zoom checks; clipping or missing script support fails |
| Spacing, rhythm and grid | Repeatable spacing roles with purposeful contrast in density; any grid break aids the brief | Alignment/grid overlay and section-to-section comparison; random spacing/novelty fails |
| Color, imagery and restraint | Legible neutral base, one accent within area budget, useful licensed imagery, no default effects | Measured color roles, contrast, screenshot, asset jobs; palette/card/gradient/glass rule violation fails |
| Interaction, states and motion | Every applicable state and recovery path clear; motion explains change and has static fallback | State matrix, keyboard story, reduced-motion/no-JS proof; fake live behavior or color-only status fails |
| Accessibility and handoff precision | Applicable WCAG 2.2 AA criteria supported and each implementation choice specified | Automated plus manual evidence and canonical checklist; missing evidence, open question or unmet AA fails |

For a wireframe review, typography means hierarchy/readability, color means grayscale restraint, and interaction means state intent. Record color/font/motion polish as **deferred to Step 6**, outside that phase's score. Do not reward visual polish before structure is approved. The handoff rubric covers all rows without those deferrals.

#### Specificity test and reference comparison

For each page, quote its actual hero line from the approved copy deck, mask the brand name and compare its meaning with two unrelated business categories. If both could use it unchanged, mark `generic-hero` FAIL. A factual descriptor, named audience job or specific proposition must make the line belong to this brief. Do not invent competitor quotations. The writer owns revisions and existing approval still applies.

Compare each page with the three inspected reference works selected in Step 3. State the exact aspect being compared, the reference evidence, where this design falls short and how the brief justifies a deliberate difference. “Looks premium” or “feels human” is not a criterion. No reference URL may be presented as inspected unless it was opened and reviewed. The current candidate contains a rubric, not a fabricated comparison against an unbuilt project.

#### Defects and the bounded loop

P0 means a serious truth, safety, access or essential task failure; P1 means a broken required rule, missing critical evidence or a build-blocking omission. P2/P3 are lesser issues only when they do not violate a BLOCK check. A required check cannot be downgraded to make a page pass.

At each round record screen/state hashes, every score and PASS/FAIL, defects with severity, evidence, owner and next correction. The designer revises; the critic evaluates the new artifact independently. Stop at three rounds maximum, or earlier when total improvement is less than one point between rounds. The stop packet lists all round scores, what changed, open defects and the decision needed from the approver. It does not authorize handoff, a fourth round or relaxed thresholds. An approval cannot waive the WCAG floor, factual truth or security rules.

The final verdict is either **PASS for the explicitly listed scope/version** or **FAIL — return to the relevant step**. A draft, an unavailable reference or a capped loop is never “pass with conditions” for Step 9.
