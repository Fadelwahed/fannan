## R16 additions (Mi'mar, 18 October 2026, panel round 16)

All browser checks below ran in Chrome 155 headless only. Panel round 16 had one reviewer (GPT), cross-checked by Mi'mar. Scope follows the threat model in `FANNAN-PLAN.md`.

- **Compositing (R16-GPT-01):** `filter: opacity()` is composited like opacity; identity filters are ignored; any other filter function, a blend mode, a backdrop filter or a mask anywhere on the text's composed ancestor chain makes that text unknown (`contrastEffects`), and the cross-check is INCOMPLETE in either rendering. Consequence: a page that keeps such an effect on text cannot pass the integrity cross-check, by design. UNTESTED: effects on pseudo-elements or SVG text; `filter` values set through `url()` to an SVG filter that is in fact an identity (counted unknown); text painted inside a canvas.
- **Native controls (R16-GPT-02):** values, placeholders (with the `::placeholder` colour), button-type input labels and a select's chosen option are contrast-checked; controls, links, images and elements with a role or ARIA label are compared with their accessible name and displayed value. The accessible name is an approximation of the specification (aria-labelledby, aria-label, label elements, value, alt, own text, title, placeholder), not the full algorithm. UNTESTED: `::file-selector-button`, date and time pickers, autofill colours, `<option>` lists while open, names from CSS generated content. As before, an unexplained JavaScript-off content difference records a content mismatch and the cross-check status is INCOMPLETE (R13 N14).
- **Wireframe coverage (R16-GPT-03):** the wireframe phase requires each screen's page to exist, an applicable default rendering per declared language, manual records per screen and language, and renders each declared screen/language with phase-appropriate checks (no frame hash, type, accent, spacing or placeholder thresholds yet). Links inside the pages (navigation between screens) are not followed.
- **Component inventory (R16-GPT-04):** the contract enforces one shared definition per component, references by id, declared variants and states, and equality of any repeated specification field; the critic records a component comparison covering every screen. Whether two instances that do not repeat a field are visually consistent is a reviewer judgment (`component-consistency`), not machine-checked.

## R15 additions (Mi'mar, 18 October 2026, panel round 15)

All browser checks below ran in Chrome 155 headless only. Panel round 15 had one reviewer (GPT); B4 was found by Mi'mar alone. Scope follows the threat model in `FANNAN-PLAN.md`.

- **Width screen (A1):** an `overflow-y: auto`/`scroll` ancestor holds lower text only when its scrollport ends inside the visible viewport (and above every clipping ancestor) and its scroll range reaches the text. UNTESTED: scrollers moved by transforms, scroll-snap or scroll-driven animation; a scroller whose visible part is cut by a sibling overlay rather than an ancestor clip.
- **Package (A2):** JSON files (by suffix, or text starting with `{` or `[`) are parsed and every key is screened with `credential_key()` (any case or separator, camelCase too), 8+ character string values; the regex stays for other text and now names access/refresh tokens and secret/access keys case-insensitively. Known limit: credentials in YAML, TOML or `.env`-style text under keys the regex does not name; JSON with comments (not parseable) falls back to the regex only.
- **Contrast (A3):** CSS `opacity` on the text and its composed ancestors is composited (premultiplied groups); a `mix-blend-mode` on the chain is counted unknown. UNTESTED: `filter: opacity()`/`brightness()`, opacity set through the Web Animations API mid-animation, `mask`, and colour-mix or relative colours that compute to non-`rgb()` strings (counted unknown, not measured).
- **Designer process (B1-B4):** the complete wireframe gate now runs inside Step 5 after the task and findability tests; column counts are unitless; `feedback-log`, `emphasis-order`, `type-measure`, `colour-roles` and `grid-per-width` are manual IDs (the last four PASS or DEFERRED in the wireframe phase); every critic check names a rubric row and the final round needs a passing check per row. The contract checks that the records exist with evidence; whether the feedback log, emphasis check, type measure, colour matrix, grid and critic judgments are good is a reviewer judgment, not machine-checked. No real project was run through the new Step 5 order.

## R13 additions (Mi'mar, 18 October 2026, panel round 13)

All browser checks below ran in Chrome 155 headless only. Scope follows the threat model in `FANNAN-PLAN.md` (honest but possibly sloppy authors; fail closed on what cannot be measured; deliberate obfuscation is a known limit, ENH in future rounds).

- **Frames (N04, N05, N21):** `iframe`, `frame`, `object`, `embed`, `fencedframe` and `portal` are nested scopes. Same-origin frames are partially inspected, for glass and width only, and every one of them fails the final handoff (DEFERRED in wireframes); there are no frame exceptions (q1). UNTESTED: no test renders a `fencedframe` or `portal`; only their presence in the selectors is checked.
- **Width screen (N01-N03, N15, N16):** body overflow that propagates to the viewport, zero-size and `display: contents` wrappers, shadow text clipped by its host, keyboard reach across a table and closed shadow roots (counted through CDP, reported as unscanned) were each reproduced and fixed. UNTESTED: a closed shadow root inside a frame; CDP counting when DevTools domains are unavailable is reported as unscanned, not exercised.
- **Stylesheets (N07):** `data:` and `blob:` stylesheets are refused; a stylesheet the probe cannot read and the checker did not serve fails. UNTESTED: `@import` chains deeper than one level in shadow roots.
- **Known limits, raw glass (N08/N09):** `\xNN`, `\uNNNN` and `\u{...}` escapes are decoded and the raw and comment-stripped text are both matched. Not covered (obfuscation, out of the threat model): string concatenation (`'backdrop-' + 'filter'`), `String.fromCharCode`, `atob`, computed property names, template literals assembled at run time, and CSS built from variables in script. The computed-style check and the JavaScript-off pass remain the defences.
- **Known limits, reduced motion (N17, N18):** the native `requestAnimationFrame` is bound at install, and `Function.prototype.call`/`apply`/`bind` and `Reflect.apply` are in the native-function check; page and states share one routine. Not covered: a page that hides patches from `Function.prototype.toString` (heuristic; the between-world comparison remains), rAF called from an `about:blank` child frame created after the init script, and animation started by timers after the 700 ms window.
- **Integrity (N11-N14, N19):** the vector now carries every hard-fail input (accent ratios, filler, rule of three, frames, unscanned stylesheets and scopes, per-width maps, contrast unknown/coverage); content compares slot and heading text; a content waiver is INCOMPLETE, never PASS; the native-function check has no default. UNTESTED: JavaScript-off equivalence for content inserted by CSS (`content:` on pseudo-elements).
- **Shadow DOM (N20):** spacing, design counters, placeholders, h1 and the copy walker use the composed tree including open shadow roots. UNTESTED: slotted light-DOM content assigned to named slots in nested shadow trees.
- **Package (N10, q2):** a credential word anywhere in a path part is refused (except three reviewed tool, test and evidence names); `tokens.json` passes only where the `handoff.json` beside it declares that path and hash and the content is design tokens; a stdlib content screen refuses obvious secrets (private-key blocks, cloud and chat tokens, OAuth secret values) in every packaged or hashed file. Known limit: secrets in formats the screen does not know, and binary files.

## R12 additions (Mi'mar, 18 October 2026, panel round 12)

All browser checks below ran in Chrome 155 headless only.

- **Accent area and stray colours (M19, closed):** the accent check binds the declared variable to the approved token only. Accent area and coverage are approximate (a DOM background-area proxy) and are decided by screenshots and the manual `accent-area` and `palette` reviews (panel A). A page that paints large areas in another, unapproved colour is NOT failed automatically. UNTESTED by design.
- **Frames (M03):** any frame (same-origin, `data:`, sandboxed or cross-origin) fails the final handoff and is recorded as DEFERRED in wireframes, because axe and the probes run in the main frame only. The R11 line below that called such frames "blocked" was wrong: they were recorded, not gated. Frame content is still not inspected (a frame is a stop, not a scan). *(Superseded by R13 N21: frames are partially inspected, for glass and width, and every frame, object, embed or fencedframe fails the handoff.)*
- **Raw glass (M04/M05):** served HTML, JavaScript and SVG count any `backdrop-filter`/`-webkit-backdrop-filter` mention not followed by `: none`, and the identifiers `backdropFilter`/`webkitBackdropFilter`; CSS escapes are resolved first; ordinary CSSOM sheets are read in the page. UNTESTED: glass built from strings at run time (`'backdrop' + 'Filter'`), and sheets whose rules the page cannot read (left to the served-file scan).
- **Integrity (M06-M08, M17):** the full hard-fail vector (glass, h1, placeholders, centred, type sizes, hero gradient, purple-blue, icon rows, accent, spacing off-scale, overflow, clipped, offscreen-left, unreachable, scrolled text, unmeasured, reflow, contrast) is compared page world vs probe world and with JavaScript off, on the positional page and on every declared state. Contrast is a custom diagnostic (text vs resolved solid background, 4.5:1 / 3:1), not axe: axe cannot run with JavaScript off. JavaScript-off content (visible copy slots, h1 count, lang, dir) must match or the cross-check is INCOMPLETE. UNTESTED: text over images or gradients in the contrast diagnostic (counted as unknown, not failed); the native-function check stays a heuristic.
- **320 px reflow (M01, M02, M09-M11, M14):** scrollers are measured with their own text, open shadow roots and same-origin frames; content outside a scroller's reachable range is `unreachable` at every width; keyboard use means an explicit `tabindex >= 0` scroller or usable targets that step across the whole extent; candidates and their exceptions are scoped to screen/state/lang. UNTESTED: closed shadow roots, `overflow: overlay`, scroll snapping, and whether a resolved candidate genuinely needs 2D layout (the reviewer's decision). *(Superseded in part by R13 N21: same-origin frames are partially inspected and fail the handoff anyway.)*
- **Reduced motion (M13):** the rAF counter is private and the wrapper non-writable; a non-finite or decreasing reading counts as a loop. UNTESTED: animation driven by timers or CSS that a page starts after the 700 ms window.
- **Spacing (M15, M20):** `html` and `body` margins are screened; every `data-fannan-spacing-exception` needs a scoped handoff `spacingExceptions` entry naming the approver. UNTESTED: exceptions on pseudo-elements.
- **Package (M12):** environment files (`.env*`), editor locks (`.#*`), credential folders (`.ssh`, `.gnupg`, `.aws`) and credential names (`credentials`, `secrets`, `service-account`, `token`, `.npmrc`, `.netrc`, `.pypirc`, `.git-credentials`) are refused with any suffix. Plural `tokens` stays allowed because design-token files are named `tokens.json`. UNTESTED: secrets inside ordinarily named files (names only; the secret grep is manual).
