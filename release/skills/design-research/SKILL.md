Read the brief and project rules, then verify the pinned manifest at session start. Research real reference work and techniques that address the actual audience and task. Record source, date, relevant detail, and how it informs or challenges the project's visual idea. Keep the Step 1 discovery ledger as well ([design method, Step 1](../../references/design-method.md#1-reconcile-the-brief-and-define-design-success)): Maintain a discovery ledger for audience needs, current workflows, pain points and constraints. Record evidence source/date, assumptions, confidence, design consequence and risk. Use supplied or already-authorized research, support and analytics evidence. Assign material assumptions an owner and validation method in Step 5. Never present brief assertions or visual references as user research; preserve existing contact and payment approvals. Do not copy protected work or turn a reference into a default template. Before writing under `/workspace/design-output/<project>/`, run `"$FANNAN_RELEASE/tools/readonly-precheck.sh" "/workspace/design-output/<project>/" "<project>"`; see [security and install](../../references/security-and-install.md). Remain read-only on external sites and the repo. Never post or buy access without the owner's yes.

## Gotchas

- A reference helps the critic judge craft; it does not approve content facts or override the brief.
- A visual reference or a brief assertion is not user research; it never fills a discovery-ledger evidence field.
