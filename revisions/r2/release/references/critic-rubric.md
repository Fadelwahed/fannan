# Critic rubric — copy and design together

The critic is independent and read-only. Load the frozen brief, rules, settings, copy/fact ledgers, reference board, scoped screen/state renders and integrated quality evidence. Do not start from the designer's self-assessment. Evaluate each page and all scoped states/locales against their real reference work; check a composition both at full-page scale and at readable 1440-pixel size. Missing evidence is FAIL, not benefit of the doubt.

## Score anchors and pass rule

Use zero-to-four scores only to make iteration visible: **0** absent or contradicted; **1** recognizable intent with major defects; **2** functional but generic or materially incomplete; **3** clear, specific and supported with all required checks passing; **4** unusually resolved craft, supported by concrete comparisons and detail. A score of four needs an explanation, not enthusiasm.

Every row and every page receives a public **PASS or FAIL**, a score, an artifact reference and a reason. PASS requires at least 3 in every applicable row, all hard checks passing and no open P0/P1. Scores are never averaged to compensate for a failed dimension. A WARN can remain only with a named owner and explicit disposition and no unanswered build question.

| Dimension | PASS at 3 requires | Evidence / automatic failure |
|---|---|---|
| Brief and point of view | One legible idea serving this audience/job, expressed consistently across the page | Brief lines for every section and deliberate decision; any orphan section fails |
| Copy specificity and truth | Accurate approved wording, identifiable offering/task and confirmed proof | Slot/source/claim ledger, project-wording checks; transferable headline, invented proof or filler fails |
| Content hierarchy and task flow | Reading order, emphasis and CTA help the named task; first impression matches actual content | Task walkthrough and annotated frame; vague hero over icon cards or misleading primary action fails |
| Typography and language | Deliberate role pairing, at most six sizes, readable measures, correct scripts/directions and uncut translations | Token-to-DOM mapping, language renders, long-text/zoom checks; clipping or missing script support fails |
| Spacing, rhythm and grid | Repeatable spacing roles with purposeful contrast in density; any grid break aids the brief | Alignment/grid overlay and section-to-section comparison; random spacing/novelty fails |
| Color, imagery and restraint | Legible neutral base, one accent within area budget, useful licensed imagery, no default effects | Measured color roles, contrast, screenshot, asset jobs; palette/card/gradient/glass rule violation fails |
| Interaction, states and motion | Every applicable state and recovery path clear; motion explains change and has static fallback | State matrix, keyboard story, reduced-motion/no-JS proof; fake live behavior or color-only status fails |
| Accessibility and handoff precision | Applicable WCAG 2.2 AA criteria supported and each implementation choice specified | Automated plus manual evidence and canonical checklist; missing evidence, open question or unmet AA fails |

For a wireframe review, typography means hierarchy/readability, color means grayscale restraint, and interaction means state intent. Record color/font/motion polish as **deferred to Step 6**, outside that phase's score. Do not reward visual polish before structure is approved. The handoff rubric covers all rows without those deferrals.

## Specificity test and reference comparison

For each page, quote its actual hero line from the approved copy deck, mask the brand name and compare its meaning with two unrelated business categories. If both could use it unchanged, mark `generic-hero` FAIL. A factual descriptor, named audience job or specific proposition must make the line belong to this brief. Do not invent competitor quotations. The writer owns revisions and existing approval still applies.

Compare each page with the three inspected reference works selected in Step 3. State the exact aspect being compared, the reference evidence, where this design falls short and how the brief justifies a deliberate difference. “Looks premium” or “feels human” is not a criterion. No reference URL may be presented as inspected unless it was opened and reviewed. The current candidate contains a rubric, not a fabricated comparison against an unbuilt project.

## Defects and the bounded loop

P0 means a serious truth, safety, access or essential task failure; P1 means a broken required rule, missing critical evidence or a build-blocking omission. P2/P3 are lesser issues only when they do not violate a BLOCK check. A required check cannot be downgraded to make a page pass.

At each round record screen/state hashes, every score and PASS/FAIL, defects with severity, evidence, owner and next correction. The designer revises; the critic evaluates the new artifact independently. Stop at three rounds maximum, or earlier when total improvement is less than one point between rounds. The stop packet lists all round scores, what changed, open defects and the decision needed from the approver. It does not authorize handoff, a fourth round or relaxed thresholds. An approval cannot waive the WCAG floor, factual truth or security rules.

The final verdict is either **PASS for the explicitly listed scope/version** or **FAIL — return to the relevant step**. A draft, an unavailable reference or a capped loop is never “pass with conditions” for Step 9.
