## Panel

q1: POSITION — Prefer protected recursive scanning of same-origin frames; fail unreadable or unsupported frames.

REASONS:

- Rejecting every frame unnecessarily blocks inspectable embedded maps and video.
- Approval requires glass, accessibility, reflow, copy, and integrity coverage inside each supported frame.
- The current blanket stop is a defensible temporary boundary until that coverage exists; an embed exemption alone would reopen M03.

q2: POSITION — A bounded design-token exception is acceptable; the current blanket plural exception is unsafe.

REASONS:

- `tokens.json` is a legitimate design-artifact name and also a plausible credential-store name.
- Permit reviewed paths only after validating design-token content; a filename alone establishes neither purpose nor safety.
- Reject arbitrary plural variants and suffixes, with regressions for both packaging inputs and changed paths.
