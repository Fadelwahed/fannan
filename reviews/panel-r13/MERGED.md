## Panel questions
**q1 — fail on any frame?**
- Agreed, 2-0: keep the blanket frame stop for now (now including object/embed/fencedframe, N04). The target is protected recursive scanning of same-origin frames, with unreadable or unsupported frames failing. Switch only once every gate (axe, focus/target size, copy, contrast, spacing, integrity vector, reduced motion) runs inside each frame, because scanning for glass alone would turn "not checked" into PASS.
- Opus adds: real maps and video are usually cross-origin, so the resource boundary already stops them. The design deliverable should be a static poster/placeholder plus an engineering note.
- Split: Opus wants a scoped, role-reviewed `frameExceptions` entry (screen/state/lang, id, reason, qaGate/approver, manual a11y evidence) in the meantime. GPT says a scoped exception cannot substitute for missing coverage. Mi'mar to decide.

**q2 — plural `tokens` exception?**
- Agreed, 2-0: **not safe as written**. Allow `tokens.json` only at the declared handoff token path **and** when its content validates as design tokens (DTCG leaves with `$value`; no `access_token`/`refresh_token`/`client_secret` keys). Refuse every other plural, suffixed, prefixed or infixed credential name.
- Opus adds a stdlib content screen for obvious secrets (`-----BEGIN`, `AKIA…`, `"refresh_token"`) on packaged files. GPT agrees it helps but says it can't replace the path+content boundary.
